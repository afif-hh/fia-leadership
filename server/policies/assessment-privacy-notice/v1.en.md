**Version 1**

## What we collect

When you take this assessment we record the answer you choose for each statement (a 1–5 scale).
These answers are held as sensitive personal data and treated as **Restricted** — access to your
raw answers is limited to the parties named below.

## What the data is used for

Your answers are processed to produce a leadership style profile that is **developmental**, not
diagnostic and not a verdict. The scores and results are an index to help you understand and
develop your leadership style — not a right/wrong judgement, and not used for academic decisions
such as progression or course marks.

## Who can access it

- **You** — you can always see your own completion status.
- **Lab Admin and the Academic Lead** — for running the assessment programme.
- **The lecturer or coach assigned to you** — only once your assessment has been completed and
  processed into a result; answers you are still filling in, and have not submitted, are visible
  to nobody but you.

Your raw answers are never written to system logs, and are not shared with anyone beyond the
parties above without separate consent (see the "Research Participation" document).

## How long it is kept

The data is kept for as long as you are enrolled as an active student on this programme, and as
required by the academic obligations of the Faculty of Administrative Science, Universitas
Brawijaya.

## Your rights

You may contact the Lab Admin with any question about this data. Agreeing to this notice is
required to start the assessment — you are free not to continue, but the assessment cannot begin
without this consent.
