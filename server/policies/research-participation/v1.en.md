# Research Participation — Optional

**Version 1**

> This is a translation. The Indonesian text of version 1 is the authoritative one; where the two
> disagree, the Indonesian wording is what governs.

Beyond your own leadership development, your assessment data may — in anonymised form — be used
for internal research by the Faculty of Administrative Science, Universitas Brawijaya into student
leadership development.

**Taking part is voluntary.** Declining research participation does NOT affect your ability to
take the assessment or to use this platform.

If you agree:

- Data used for research is anonymised or aggregated before it is analysed.
- You can withdraw this consent at any time from your profile page; answers you have already given
  are not deleted, but your data will no longer be included in research exports made after the
  withdrawal.

Tick the box on the consent page only if you agree to your data being used for this research.
